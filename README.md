## 🧼 Textile Care Protocols

1. **Washing:** Cold wash cycle (≤ 30°C / 86°F) inside out with neutral pH detergents.
2. **Drying:** Air dry flat to maintain horizontal thread tension and prevent mockneck curling.
3. **Storage:** Fold garments flat; avoid wire hangers to preserve shoulder architecture.
